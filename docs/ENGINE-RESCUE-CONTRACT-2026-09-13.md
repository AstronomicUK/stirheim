# Engine rescue integration contract

Source: reference/rules/02-weapons-armour-equipment.md, Engine of Chaos, Prisoners (around line 2288). Custody and journeys are already connected; this document concerns rescue during a battle. Codex is taking this forward while Claude's earlier contract-review request remains unacknowledged.

## Saved facts

Each match has a rescue record per present Engine. It snapshots prisoners held when the Engine joins that battle, their names, source custody IDs and former warbands. These are temporary battle participants, never new permanent roster entries. Non-participating prisoners receive the basic Human Warrior profile; participating prisoners retain their original profiles. Their confiscated kit remains in the Chaos Dwarf stash.

Prison keys belong to the Chaos Dwarf warband, not a particular prisoner. Taking a Gaoler out of action grants keys to the model responsible. Record the source combat event, or a reasoned tabletop confirmation. If that key holder is taken out of action, keys pass to the model responsible; an unknown keeper remains unresolved rather than silently being assigned. More than one Gaoler can supply keys.

A key holder in base contact may free an Engine's held prisoners. Base contact is a tabletop fact requiring confirmation. Destruction frees them without keys. Freed prisoners move towards the nearest table edge; the UI records whether they escaped. A Chaos Dwarf rout before release leaves held prisoners captured. There must be no invented automatic proximity, escape, OOA or reward.

## Persistence and authority

Match participants and GM can read the battle facts. A player records actions of their own model; the Engine owner or GM records destruction and rout facts. Changes affecting a named prisoner's permanent roster use the existing two-player consent machinery. A mere click marking a prisoner freed must not silently alter an absent player's roster. All corrections retain prior facts and block changes with dependent accepted outcomes.

## Outcomes needing explicit handling

Escaped named prisoners return to their former warbands, with confiscated kit staying behind. Anonymous prisoners leave custody without a fabricated owner roster. Henchmen restore one individual with their original profile; do not restore their confiscated kit or merge incompatible profiles. The current Hero slot or warband-size limits must not erase an existing rescued model.

The source does not explicitly specify the aftermath of a freed prisoner who has not reached an edge when the battle ends, nor a freed prisoner taken out of action before escaping. Preserve these as unresolved outcomes for a table ruling instead of choosing death, recapture or guaranteed escape. These do not block recording keys, contact, release or a completed escape.
